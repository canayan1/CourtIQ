# CourtIQ Free vs Premium

## Free experience
The free app delivers the core tennis decision training workflow:
- Daily five-question scenario quiz
- Instant answer feedback with coaching explanations
- Category practice quizzes for Serve, Return, Rally, Net Play, and Mental
- Persistent daily streak tracking
- Daily training tip or takeaway
- Local on-device experience with no backend dependency

## Premium experience (future)
Premium should add depth without changing the core coaching promise:
- Full mistake mapping that translates quiz patterns into focused practice plans
- Premium training guides for movement, conditioning, and mental routines
- Expanded daily training tips and personalized focus areas
- Historical performance summaries and confidence-based progress recommendations
- Premium should still feel practical, not flashy

## What stays free
- All core decision training content
- Daily quiz flow and practice quiz access
- Local streak and stats tracking
- Coaching explanation for every question

## Premium value statement
Premium is for players who want to go beyond awareness and practice the exact skills behind the mistake:
- “Your decisions are good. Now train the habits that will make them automatic.”

## V1 vs V2 boundaries
V1:
- Local quiz engine, daily question bank, practice categories, streaks, result feedback
- No premium gating or subscriptions
- No backend or remote sync

V2:
- Mistake-to-training mapping
- Structured movement/conditioning guidance
- Mental training routines and match preparation plans
- Premium guidance and advanced progress insights
