# Instagram welcome DM — new followers

Audience: people who follow Tennis Ireland / WTN accounts and followed back.
Mostly club and improving adult players, rating-literate, Ireland-heavy.

Voice: the account's, not a personal note. No name, no founder story, no
"I built this". Professional register — no exclamation marks, no hype.

Rules this copy keeps:
- No implied affiliation with Tennis Ireland, the ITF or WTN.
- No user counts, ratings, awards or testimonials we don't have.
- Form analysis is labelled beta, because it is beta in the app.
- Coach reviews are described as in development, never as available, and
  never with a date.

## D. Replies you'll get

**"Is there an Android version?"**
> Not yet — iOS only for now. Send an email address and we'll tell you the
> day Android lands, nothing else.

**"What does it cost?"**
> Free. The scenarios, wall practice, the match journal and the doubles side
> don't cost anything. The only paid part is the AI coach, because every
> message costs real money to run.

**"How accurate is the form analysis?"**
> It's beta, and we label it that way in the app. It reads a filmed swing
> and gives you a score and a few specifics. It won't replace a coach
> watching you — which is why coach reviews are what we're building next.

**"Real coaches — when?"**
> In development. We'd rather ship it late than ship it thin, so no date
> yet. Say the word and we'll message you when it opens.

**"Are you connected to Tennis Ireland / WTN?"**
> No — no affiliation. DropVolley is independent.

**"I tried it, here's what I didn't like…"**
> Name the specific thing that will change, change it, and message them back
> when it ships. That is the whole point of this campaign.
