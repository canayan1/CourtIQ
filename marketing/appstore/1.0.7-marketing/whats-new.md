# What's New — 1.0.7 (build 36)

## en-US

DropVolley now has four tabs, one for each thing it does.

• Tactics — a new tab. Thirty lessons that teach match tactics one decision at a time, each with its own court diagram. Chapter 1 is free, and every player gets one free lesson a day.
• Wall — rebuilt. The camera now counts your reps by watching you turn and swing (no sound, no tripod). Stroke reading is still beta; tell us when it gets one wrong.
• Coach — your video, reviewed. AI reads your swing today; the real-coach review is on a waitlist you can join inside.
• Home — Drills, Matches, Doubles, Recover and Programs are one tap away.
