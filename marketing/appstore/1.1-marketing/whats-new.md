# What's New — 1.1 (build 37)

## en-US

DropVolley now does three things, and every screen says which one you're in.

• Home shows today's three moves: your last swing read, your next wall rung, your next tactics lesson.
• Onboarding shows the three things before it asks you anything, and ends with your first week.
• Coach: film a swing, read what the coach sees, then ask about that read. The real-coach review stays on the waitlist until it's real.
• Wall: rep counting now reads your whole upper body, so a swing that crosses in front of you still counts and is called correctly.
• Tactics: lessons speak the app's language now, in the app's own components.
• One membership, three reasons — the paywall says exactly what Premium adds to each.
• Lighter app: screens that no longer existed are gone.
