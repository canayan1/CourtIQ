# What's New — 1.4 (build 39)

Scope note: this release is the head-to-head challenge and one fix. Nothing
from the sensing work (belt-phone session, Apple Watch, bench card, trends)
ships here — it is DEBUG-only on main and has not met a court or a wrist. The
GA4 screen-tracking commit is in this build and is invisible to players, so it
is not announced.

## en-US

Challenge a friend to the questions you just played.

• After your Daily IQ, send a challenge link. Your friend plays the same questions — same order, same scenarios — and sees your score next to theirs.
• They don't need the app open to receive it: the link opens DropVolley, or the App Store if they don't have it yet.
• Fixed: sharing from the app now points to DropVolley on the App Store.
