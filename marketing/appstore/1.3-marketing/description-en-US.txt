DESCRIPTION
The smartest tennis players aren't the best athletes. They're the best decision-makers.

Anyone can film a forehand. The players who win know which shot, which moment, which risk — and keep their head when it's tight. That's Tennis IQ. It's the part every other app leaves out. DropVolley trains it — and now it does four things, each with its own tab.

COACH — YOUR SWING, REVIEWED
Film a forehand, backhand, serve, volley or your footwork. The AI reads it frame by frame — preparation and grip, contact point, follow-through, balance — and gives you clear strengths and priority fixes, like a coach watching the tape with you. A review by a real, certified coach — voice notes, timestamped, back within 72 hours — is coming; the waitlist is inside the app.

WALL — THE WALL NEVER MISSES. NOW IT COUNTS.
Eight levels of wall drills, from a steady rally to serve-plus-one patterns. Prop your phone behind you and the camera watches you turn and swing, counts every rep on-device, reads the ball against the net line, and grades the rung green, gold or try again. No sound, no tripod, no video leaves your phone. Stroke reading is beta and improving.

TACTICS — TAUGHT LIKE A LANGUAGE
Thirty lessons that teach match tactics one decision at a time — court geometry, rally patterns, serve patterns, reading your opponent — each with its own court diagram, then a quick check that you got it. Chapter 1 is free, and every player gets one free lesson a day.

JOURNAL — YOUR SEASON, A DAY AT A TIME
Your matches and what you ate, in one calendar. Log a match in seconds, keyboard-free, with the result, your ratings and the one thing you'd do differently. Log the last meal before you play and rate energy, legs, focus and stomach after — once there's enough of your own data, it tells you which of your habits went with better legs. Tap any day, today or three weeks back, and fill it in; days you didn't play count too. The app asks the follow-up instead of waiting, and "same as last time" fills the whole form from your last entry. A nutrition guide with every claim sourced comes with it, and 12 recipes are part of Premium.
