## Video direction

The ad argues by withholding, so the motion withholds too. Three of the five
frames have no camera move at all; the one push-in, in frame 3, is the
payoff and gets the only real movement in the piece. Reveals are paced to
**reading speed**, not to a voiceover — there is no narration, viewers watch
muted, and the on-screen copy is the whole script. A line that cannot be
read in its window is a line that has to get shorter.

Rhythm: frames 1 and 4 are held type on ink, frame 3 is the single sustained
move, frames 2 and 5 are light cascades on cream. Ink · cream · cream · ink
· cream keeps the two grounds alternating so the piece has a pulse without
any effect doing the work.

Nothing bounces. Every settle is a long-tail curve. No shake, no whip, no
glow, no stock footage, no purple-blue gradients — the brand is a clay court
and cream paper, and the product's whole claim is restraint.

The BGM is a quiet bed with no build and no drop; it should be unnoticeable
if the viewer has sound on and irrelevant if they do not.
