# Asset inventory

No website was captured — DropVolley is an iOS app and the brief sets
`capture: none`. These are real simulator captures of the shipping 1.3
build, 1206×2622, taken from `marketing/instagram/shots/`.

## capture/assets/fuel-insights.png — HERO
The Nutrition screen with data in it. Shows, in order: the "Fuel log" eyebrow
and headline "What you ate, and how you played"; a gold card reading "You
logged what you ate and never said how it went. Four taps." with a "Rate how
you felt" button; a clay "Log before you play" button; and the payoff — a
"WHAT WORKS FOR YOU" card with "Across 3 rated sessions", four averages
(Energy 3.0, Legs 3.0, Focus 3.7, Stomach 3.0) and the line the whole ad is
built on: "About 7 more rated sessions until your first comparison. Each one
needs at least 5 sessions on each side to mean anything."

## capture/assets/fuel-log.png
The log sheet. Single-choice chips: "Practice, match, or a day off?" with
Practice / Match / Wall / Didn't play; "When was your last real meal?" with
five timing chips; "What was it, roughly?" with five meal shapes; water;
caffeine; an optional note field; Save.

## capture/assets/journal-cal.png
The Tennis Journal tab: seven-week calendar, clay squares with a checkmark
for days played, moss squares with a fork for days with a fuel entry, a
corner dot where a day has both. Legend: Played · Fuel logged.
