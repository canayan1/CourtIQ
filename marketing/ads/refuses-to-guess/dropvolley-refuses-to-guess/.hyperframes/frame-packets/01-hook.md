Scene 1 (0.0–1.6s): ink ground, empty. "Every nutrition app tells you what
to eat." builds line by line, centred, each line its own move on a long-tail
settle — Centered template, type at ~55% of frame, nothing else on screen.
Scene 2 (1.6–2.6s): full stop. Everything holds dead still — no drift, no
breathing. The stillness is the beat; it reads as the sentence landing.
Scene 3 (2.6–4.0s): "This one won't." cuts in beneath, smaller, clay, on a
hard cut rather than a fade — the contradiction should arrive, not dissolve.
Held to the end.

Ink ground, no image. "Every nutrition app tells you what to eat." lands
first, in cream, sentence case, three lines. A beat of stillness — nothing
moves, which is unusual in this feed and is the point. Then, smaller and in
clay, on its own line: "This one won't."

The hook works because it is a promise being withdrawn. Nobody in this
category opens by saying what they will not do.
