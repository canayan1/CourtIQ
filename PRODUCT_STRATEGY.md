# CourtIQ Product Strategy

## Purpose
CourtIQ is a local-first tennis improvement app for newer, committed club players and aspiring intermediates. The product helps players convert match decisions into tangible practice habits through curated decision-making scenarios, simple feedback, and daily training guidance.

## Core value proposition
- Make tennis decision-making feel like coaching, not a trivia quiz.
- Connect every question to a practical correction or training tip.
- Keep the product lightweight and local-first for easy release and iteration.

## Target user
- Recreational players who want to improve court sense
- Club players who practice with purpose
- Tennis camp participants looking for daily training structure
- Beginners who have learned the basics and now need better decision habits

## High-level product pillars
1. Decision engine
   - Daily scenario-based quizzes
   - Instant right/wrong feedback with coaching explanations
   - Post-quiz training guidance and next-step advice
2. Performance engine
   - Fitness and movement guidance tied to court outcomes
   - Simple strength and conditioning rules that support tennis speed and stamina
3. Mental engine
   - Practical rituals and focus patterns for match resilience
   - Guidance on pressure, routines, and momentum management

## V1 focus
- Launch with the decision engine as the primary experience
- Local daily quiz sessions and category practice
- Daily training tip or coaching takeaway after each session
- Minimal UI: Today, Practice, Stats
- No backend, no authentication, no monetization

## Success criteria for V1
- Players can complete a 5-question daily scenario quiz and receive real coaching feedback
- The app surfaces one clear daily focus area from the quiz content
- The experience feels like training, not a generic quiz
- The product is stable on-device with deterministic daily content

## Future V2 direction
- Add deeper recommendation logic mapping quiz mistakes to training micro-workouts
- Build a lightweight performance engine for movement, conditioning, and court-specific drills
- Add practical mental training modules and match-day rituals
- Add premium guidance paths and richer progress insights
