# Influencer Outreach Tracker

Goal: get **20 micro-influencers in the tennis space to try CourtIQ during beta** so 1–2 of them mention it on launch day.

**Realistic funnel**: 20 contacted → 5 reply → 2 actually try → 1–2 share on launch day. Don't expect more.

---

## Target profile

- **Audience size**: 5k–200k followers/subscribers (micro-influencers — bigger ones won't reply or will demand payment).
- **Niche**: club/recreational tennis, technique tips, strategy, training, gear reviews. **Not** pros or pure ATP/WTA news.
- **Languages**: English (global) and Turkish (local).
- **Active**: posted in the last 30 days.

## Where to find them

- **YouTube**: search "tennis tips", "tennis strategy", "tennis IQ", "club player tennis", "tennis drills" — sort by views/relevance, filter to last year.
- **Instagram**: hashtags #tennistips #tennisstrategy #teniscoach #tennisdrills #tenis #tenishocası #recreationaltennis
- **TikTok**: same hashtags.
- **Twitter/X**: search "tennis coach", "tennis instructor", "I teach tennis"
- **Reddit**: power posters in r/tennis, r/10s
- **Local**: tennis academies in İstanbul, Ankara, İzmir — most have an Instagram presence

## Follow-up rule

If no reply in **7 days**, send **one** follow-up:

```
Hi [name] — just bumping this in case it slipped past. No pressure,
total respect if it's not the right fit. Beta link is still open:
[link]. — [your name]
```

After the follow-up, **stop**. Move on.

---

## What to track weekly

- Total contacted
- Total replied
- Total installed
- Total mentioned at launch

If after 1 week you've contacted 20 and gotten 0 replies, **the message is wrong**, not the audience. Rewrite it, try again with a fresh 10.
