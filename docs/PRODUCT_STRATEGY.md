# CourtIQ Product Strategy

## Purpose

CourtIQ is a tennis improvement app for committed club players who want sharper decisions, more structured training, and a cleaner weekly routine. The product connects decision-making, physical work, recovery, and focused discussion in one system.

## Core value proposition

- Make tennis decision-making feel like coaching, not a trivia quiz.
- Connect every question to practical training and recovery work.
- Keep community discussion anchored to actual content instead of generic social noise.
- Let serious users sign in, sync progress, and carry premium access across devices.

## Target user

- Recreational players who want to improve court sense
- Club players who practice with purpose
- Tennis camp participants looking for weekly structure
- Players who want one place for IQ, training, mobility, and accountability

## Current launch pillars

1. Decision engine
   - Daily scenario-based quizzes
   - Instant right/wrong feedback with coaching explanations
   - Premium practice skill blocks
2. Performance engine
   - Free foundation training plan
   - Premium sport-specific training tracks
   - Weekly persistence checks and history
3. Recovery engine
   - Mobility previews in free mode
   - Full recovery library in All Access
4. Accountability engine
   - Sign in with Apple profile sync
   - Content-linked discussion threads
   - Manual moderation workflow for reports

## Launch success criteria

- Players can complete a 5-question daily scenario quiz and receive real coaching feedback.
- Signed-in users can relaunch and recover synced profile/progress state.
- Premium entitlements accurately control practice, training, mobility, archive, and community posting surfaces.
- Community stays useful because it is tied to specific quizzes, plans, and flows.
- The product feels intentional and stable enough for TestFlight/App Store review.
