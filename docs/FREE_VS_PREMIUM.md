# CourtIQ Free vs Premium

## Free experience

The free app delivers the daily habit loop and a real preview of the wider system:
- Daily five-question scenario quiz
- Instant answer feedback with coaching explanations
- The free 8-week hybrid foundation plan
- Two preview mobility flows
- Read-only community threads
- Local guest mode with on-device progress

## Premium experience

All Access adds depth without changing the core coaching promise:
- Premium practice skill blocks
- Premium training tracks for footwork, stronger shots, flexibility, and match conditioning
- The full mobility and recovery library
- Archived quiz history and focus insights
- Premium community posting, likes, and reports
- Signed-in sync across devices

## What stays free

- The Today hub and daily quiz flow
- Guest onboarding and on-device progress
- Free foundation training
- Community reading

## Premium value statement

Premium is for players who want to go beyond awareness and practice the exact habits behind the mistake:
- “Sharpen the decision, then train the body and recovery around it.”
