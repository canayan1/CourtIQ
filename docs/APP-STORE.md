## Description (≤4000 chars)
**Most tennis apps track scores. DropVolley makes you smarter on court.**

DropVolley is your personal AI tennis coach — for the part of the game lessons rarely reach: your swing mechanics, your shot decisions, and your match craft. Film, log, ask, and improve.

**🎾 AI Swing Analysis**
Film any swing — forehand, backhand, serve, volley, or a whole session — and get a 0–100 score plus frame-by-frame coaching: what's working, what to fix, and one thing to try next. Save every analysis and watch your score climb.

**📋 AI Match Coaching**
Log a match in seconds. DropVolley reads your result, self-ratings, and notes, then tells you exactly what to work on before your next one — like a coach who watched you play.

**🧠 Pre-match mental check**
A 30-second mental read before you walk on — energy, confidence, nerves — turned into a concrete settle-down routine and a focus cue. Play calmer, sharper.

**👥 Doubles compatibility**
See how you and your partner actually fit as a team — a compatibility score plus a game plan (formation, who poaches, who covers the middle). Invite your partner in one tap and build your pairing together.

**💬 AI Coach**
Ask anything — tactics, technique, match prep. The coach already knows your profile, your matches, and your quizzes, so the advice is about YOUR game.

**⚡ Daily drills + Tennis IQ quizzes**
Scenario quizzes that sharpen real match decisions, a daily court-tap drill, mobility flows, and structured training blocks. A little every day adds up.

**📈 Your Tennis Profile**
A quick self-assessment builds your level, play-style, strengths, and growth areas — and personalizes everything above.

**Who it's for**
Adult players from beginner to solid club level who want to actually get better — not just log scores.

**Premium**
Unlock the full AI coaching suite with a subscription. Try it and see your first insight before you commit.

_Coaching guidance is for performance improvement, not medical advice._
