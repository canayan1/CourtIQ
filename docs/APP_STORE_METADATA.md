# CourtIQ — App Store Metadata

**Positioning:** A reflective player tool for tennis players who think about their game. Built around four daily rituals (tap drill, pro shot, match logging, mobility) instead of passive content.

**Target user:** Club / intermediate / advanced recreational tennis player who plays 1-4 times a week, watches the pro tour, and wants to *improve the way they think on court* — not just hit more balls.

**Tone:** Direct. No marketing fluff. The features speak for themselves.

## Description (English — 4,000 char max)

```
CourtIQ is a quiet, focused tool for tennis players who think.

Most tennis apps want to count your steps or sell you a workout. CourtIQ does something different: it trains the part of your game between points — the part that decides whether your forehand goes back in or wins you the rally.

Four daily rituals, no time waste:

DAILY COURT DRILL — 30 seconds
Five tactical scenarios. Tap the smartest shot. See the ideal zone. Share the result.

PRO SHOT OF THE DAY
One animated pro pattern, every day. Federer's serve+1. Nadal's banana forehand. Djokovic's backhand down-the-line change. Watch how the best players construct points — and save them to your library.

MATCH JOURNAL
Log your matches the way you actually play them. Pre-match plan. Post-match notes. Quick 1-5 ratings for serve, return, movement, and mental. Your trend builds over weeks.

ACTIVITY RINGS
Drill · Match · Mobility. Close all three for a Triple Ring Day. Watch them stack up over the season.

Plus what we already knew tennis players wanted:

— 8-week training programs (PHEC: plyometric + hypertrophy + explosive + conditioning) designed around how tennis players actually move
— A mobility library for warm-ups, resets, and recovery
— Your tennis avatar, customizable across outfits, rackets, courts, and accents — gear unlocks as you log streaks, complete drills, and hit triple rings

Free forever:
— Daily Court Drill
— Pro Shot of the Day
— Match Journal & Quick Log
— Three Rings
— Foundation training plan
— Mobility previews
— Your customizable avatar (starter gear)

All Access (premium subscription):
— Premium 8-week training tracks: footwork, stronger shots, flexibility, match conditioning
— Full mobility library
— Archived quiz history and trend insights
— Premium community participation

Built in Ireland by a club tennis player who got tired of generic fitness apps. Available in English and Turkish.

— No ads.
— No data sold.
— No social feed.
— No notification spam.

A tool for the part of tennis you can practice without a court.
```

## Screenshots — frame strategy

Per device size, produce 5-6 frames. iPhone 6.7" (1290 × 2796) is mandatory; iPhone 6.1" is recommended.

Recommended order — first 3 are visible on the search-results card, so they matter most:

1. **Daily Court Tap Drill — mid-game** (the unique format nobody else has)
   Visual: court diagram + tap dot + zone reveal
   Caption: "Tap the smartest shot. Every day."

2. **Pro Shot of the Day — animation**
   Visual: animated court mid-pattern, "Federer · Serve+1" title
   Caption: "Watch the pros, every day."

3. **Three Activity Rings + Match Journal entry**
   Visual: rings card + recent match list
   Caption: "Close your rings. Log your matches."

4. **Tennis Avatar — Locker Room**
   Visual: avatar in locker room with unlocked gear
   Caption: "Build your court look."

5. **8-week Training Program — PHEC**
   Visual: training program card + week strip + exercise list
   Caption: "Tennis-specific strength + conditioning."

6. **Mobility — Quick Reset Flow**
   Visual: grass-court hero + figure motif + numbered movement list
   Caption: "Move better between points."

Frame production notes:
- Use Figma or similar to add a colored backdrop matching `AppPalette.cream`
- White caption typography in `SF Pro Rounded Heavy`, ~36pt
- Caption sits in the top ~20% of the frame, screenshot in the bottom 80%
- Same backdrop + caption position across all 6 frames = brand-consistent
