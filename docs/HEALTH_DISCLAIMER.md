# Health & Safety Disclaimer

**Last updated:** May 2026

Please read this carefully before starting any training program, mobility flow, or tennis-related physical activity in CourtIQ.

## CourtIQ is not medical advice

CourtIQ provides general tennis education, training programs, mobility content, and decision-making scenarios. It is **not** medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is not a substitute for a qualified physician, physiotherapist, certified strength and conditioning specialist, or tennis coach.

## Consult a professional before you start

You should speak to a qualified medical professional before beginning any new physical activity — including anything you find in CourtIQ — especially if you:

- Have any heart, lung, joint, or musculoskeletal condition;
- Have high blood pressure or are taking medication;
- Are recovering from surgery, injury, or illness;
- Are pregnant or recently gave birth;
- Are over 65, or have not exercised regularly in the past 6 months;
- Experience any symptom that could be aggravated by physical activity.

If you are not sure whether you should attempt the activities in CourtIQ, **don't** — ask your doctor first.

## Stop if something feels wrong

If you experience pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, nausea, chest discomfort, or any unusual symptoms during or after using CourtIQ, **stop immediately** and seek medical attention.

## You assume the risk

Tennis, strength training, plyometrics, mobility work, and conditioning carry an inherent risk of injury — including serious injury, permanent disability, or death. By using CourtIQ, you acknowledge and accept that risk.

You are responsible for:

- Evaluating your own fitness level;
- Performing all movements with safe technique and appropriate load;
- Warming up before exercise and cooling down after;
- The safety of your equipment, court, gym, footwear, and environment;
- Adequate hydration, nutrition, and rest;
- Recognizing your own limits and stopping when you should.

## No supervision

CourtIQ is self-directed. You are not supervised by a coach or trainer through the app. Form cues and exercise prescriptions are general guidance — not personalized instruction.
